# Nova Home

## Installation
1.  Enable "Install unknown apps" in your phone's settings.
2.  Transfer the debug APK to your Honor X9d.
3.  Open the file manager, tap the APK, and follow the prompts to install.

## Reliability Settings
To ensure Nova Home runs reliably on MagicOS:
1.  Open Nova Home Settings.
2.  Tap "Reliability Settings".
3.  Enable "Unrestricted" battery usage.
4.  Enable notifications.
5.  Lock the app in your "Recent Apps" screen (look for a lock icon).
