# Privacy Policy for Nova Home Launcher

**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

Nova Home ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Home handles your information when you use our Android launcher application.

## 1. Information We Access
Nova Home operates locally on your device. Depending on the features you choose to enable, the app may access:
*   **Installed Applications:** To display your app drawer and organize your installed applications.
*   **Device Contacts (Optional):** If you enable contact search, the app queries local device contacts solely to provide search results within the launcher. This data is never stored or transmitted.
*   **Calendar Events (Optional):** If you enable calendar widgets, the app reads upcoming calendar entries locally to display them in the "At a Glance" widget.
*   **Device Location (Optional):** Used locally to fetch weather information for your current area via public weather APIs.
*   **Notifications (Optional):** If you enable unread notification badges, the app accesses notification counts locally on your device.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
*   **No Data Collection:** Nova Home does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, crash reports, or telemetry to external servers.
*   **No Third-Party Ads:** The app contains no advertisements or third-party trackers.
*   **Local Storage:** All preferences, hidden app lists, and layouts are stored securely on your device using Android DataStore and Room Database.

## 3. Data Security
All data accessed by Nova Home stays strictly on your device. Because we do not collect your data on any servers, there is no risk of cloud data breaches from our end.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document.
